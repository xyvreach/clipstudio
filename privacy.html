Clip Studio 369

Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Clip Studio 369 ("the app") is a personal desktop tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It has no servers or user database of its own. This policy explains what TikTok data the app uses when you connect a TikTok account, and what happens to it.

What the app receives from TikTok

Only after you approve it on TikTok's own sign-in page:

TikTok permissionDataWhy
user.info.basicAccount ID, display name, profile pictureShow which account is connected
user.info.profileUsername, profile linkLabel the account and link to it
user.info.statsFollower, like and video countsShow account growth in the app
video.listIDs and view counts of your public videosShow how posted clips perform
video.uploadNone read; lets the app send a video to your draftsUpload clips for you to finish and post

The app never receives your TikTok password, messages, contacts, or private videos.

Where it is stored

Everything above, plus the access token TikTok issues, is stored only in a local file on the computer running the app. It is not uploaded to any server, sold, shared with advertisers, or used to build profiles.

Other services

To write captions, the app can send frames or transcripts of the videos you choose to edit to an AI text service. No TikTok account data is sent to it.

How long it is kept

Until you disconnect. Disconnecting in the app deletes the stored token and account details. You can also revoke access in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions, after which the app can no longer read or upload anything.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
